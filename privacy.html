A Late Night Reborn Discord

Privacy Notice

A Late Night Reborn is a community run Final Fantasy XIV raid server. This page explains what information we collect when you sign up for runs, why we need it, and how it is handled.

Data Controller: A Late Night Reborn community administration & raid leadership team

badge 1. What Information We Use

When you sign in with Discord or fill out a run signup on the site, we only look at what our hosts actually need to put together raid rosters:

  • Discord Account: Your Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar (via Discord's basic identify permission). We never ask for or receive your email, friends list, or private messages.
  • Character Name: If you have the verified role in our Discord server, we automatically read your in-game character name from your server nickname so you don't have to retype it. We do not store or track any information about your home world or data center.
  • Raid Preferences: The jobs and roles you can play, your progression point, your party preference, an optional friend or group code, and any notes you leave for the host.

track_changes 2. Why We Need This Information

Everything we collect is used strictly to run community raids:

  • Balancing party comps and roles for scheduled events (Forked Tower: Blood, Forked Tower: Magic, Chaotic Alliance Raids, and Baldesion Arsenal).
  • Pinging and messaging confirmed players on Discord when rosters are ready.
  • Checking that you are in our Discord server so hosts have a way to contact you for your run.

We will never sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with commercial third parties, advertisers, or marketing agencies.

cloud_off 3. Where Your Data Lives

We do not store user accounts, passwords, or emails on our web server. When you submit a signup, the details are sent directly over an encrypted connection to our raid planning Google Sheet.

The website server only passes the form submission straight to the sheet and does not store copies of your submissions.

Your login session confirms your Discord identity while you're using the site. It stays active as long as you visit regularly, and automatically expires after 7 days of inactivity.

group 4. Who Can See Your Information & Technical Services

Access to submitted signup details is limited strictly to our raid lead team. Final rosters posted publicly in Discord channels only show the character names, assigned jobs, and parties needed for the raid.

Technical Infrastructure Providers:

To run community signups and coordinate events, we rely on two trusted services acting as technical infrastructure processors on our behalf:

  • Discord Inc.: For user authentication (OAuth2 identity) and event announcements / run pings.
  • Google LLC (Google Workspace): Provides the secure cloud spreadsheet (Google Sheets) where event rosters are compiled and balanced by raid leaders.

history 5. How Long We Keep It

Signup entries are only kept for as long as needed to organize and finish that week's runs.

Old signup sheets from past content weeks are cleared out once those events are over.

cookie 6. Cookies & Local Storage

We only use a few basic browser items to keep you signed in and remember your settings:

  • Login Session: An encrypted cookie that keeps you logged in while using the site. It automatically stays active as long as you visit regularly, and is cleared immediately when you sign out.
  • Discord Login Security: A temporary security code stored in your browser while logging in through Discord to protect against login tampering. It is automatically deleted once you are signed in.
  • Theme Preference: Remembers whether you prefer dark or light mode across visits.

There are zero advertising cookies, third-party analytics trackers, or surveillance scripts on this website.

verified_user 7. Your Rights & Deleting Your Data

You always have full control over your information:

  • Check Your Signups: You can view all your active signups right on the website whenever you are signed in.
  • Update Your Details: If your job, role, or availability changes, let your hosts know or update your signup before rosters lock.
  • Delete Your Data: You can request complete removal of your signup submissions from our planning sheets at any time.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: If you believe your data has been handled improperly under GDPR or applicable data protection regulations, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority (such as the UK ICO or your national EU/EEA regulator).
How to Request Changes or Deletion: Simply open a Ticket on our Discord server at https://discord.gg/alnr. Our staff will take care of it right away.
