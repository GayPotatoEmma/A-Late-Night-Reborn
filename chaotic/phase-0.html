Phase 0

Overview

Phase 0 serves as the brief opening sequence of The Cloud of Darkness (Chaotic). The raid handles two initial Blade of Darkness casts before mitigating a heavy raidwide hit from Deluge of Darkness, which transitions the encounter directly into Phase 1.

Blade of Darkness

At the start of the encounter, the boss delivers a single auto-attack before immediately casting Blade of Darkness.

  • Raised Hand: If she raises one of her hands, move to the opposite side of the arena to avoid a large donut-shaped AoE.
  • Dual Blades: If she braces herself and summons two blades directly in front of her, move away from the boss to avoid a point-blank AoE.

Getting hit by either variation inflicts a 15-second physical Bleed debuff. Tanks take minimal direct damage.

✦
Tanks and melee players can use personal mitigation and defensive cooldowns to comfortably maximize uptime during these casts.

Deluge of Darkness

Following the two casts of Blade of Darkness, the boss casts Deluge of Darkness, a massive raidwide attack that inflicts a heavy Bleed debuff across the entire raid.

  • Target Mitigation: Ensure at least Reprisal, Feint, and Addle are applied to the boss before the cast finishes.
  • Party Mitigation: Individual parties should supplement with additional party-wide mitigation and shields to survive the initial hit and tick damage.
