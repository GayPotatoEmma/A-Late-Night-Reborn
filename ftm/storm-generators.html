Storm Generators

Overview

Three Teleportation Sigils at the entrance transport players up to three separate elevated platforms. Each platform contains a Storm Generator that must be destroyed.

  • Raid Split Assignments:
    • Parties A & 1: Left Platform
    • Parties B & 2: Middle Platform
    • Parties C & 3: Right Platform
  • Thunderclap Concerto:
    • The Storm Generator periodically casts Thunderclap Concerto. Interrupt this spell every single time it is cast.
  • Enrage:
    • Hold DPS once your Storm Generator reaches 5% HP. All three Generators must die at roughly the same time.
    • When the first Generator dies, the remaining Generators trigger a 15-second enrage cast that will wipe the entire raid if allowed to finish.

Cyclops

During the encounter, two Cyclops add spawns appear on both the Left and Right platforms.

  • Circle AoEs:
    • Players on the Left and Right platforms cannot see the Cyclops adds until they begin attacking with massive circle AoEs.
    • The AoEs leave only a small cardinal safe spot (either North/South or East/West).
  • Safe Spot Callouts:
    • Players on the Middle platform can see the Cyclops adds from the moment they spawn.
    • Middle platform players must immediately call out the safe spots for the Left and Right teams. Because safe spots may differ between platforms, listen closely to your assigned callout.

Slimes

Tower Slimes periodically spawn along the North edge of each platform.

  • Tower Slimes:
    • Tanks must grab the Tower Slimes and drag them onto the Storm Generator so they can be cleaved down alongside the main target.
    • Tanks should use strong defensive cooldowns, as the slimes hit surprisingly hard.
  • The First Song:
    • Tower Slimes on the Middle platform cast The First Song.
    • A Phantom Bard must interrupt this cast using Romeo's Ballad to stun the slimes. If the cast finishes, it will result in a raid wipe.
